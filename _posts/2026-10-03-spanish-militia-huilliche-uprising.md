## Simple Sanskrit notes for learners — Theme: Uprising and Suppression
- विद्रोहः (vidrohaḥ) — rebellion, uprising; literal sense "rising against". [rebellion / 起义]
- सेना (senā) — army, militia; literal sense "armed force". [army / 军队]
- दमनम् (damanam) — suppression, quelling; literal sense "pressing down". [suppression / 镇压]

## English
- On October 3, 1792, a militia departed from the Spanish stronghold of Valdivia to quell a Huilliche uprising in southern Chile.
- The event exposed the fragile hold of colonial rule, maintained only through repeated military expeditions against indigenous peoples.
- The lesson endures: force can suppress a revolt, but it cannot resolve the grievances that cause one.
