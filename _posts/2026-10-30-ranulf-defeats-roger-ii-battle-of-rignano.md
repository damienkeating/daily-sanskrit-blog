## Sanskrit news

- ११३७ क्रिस्ताब्दे अक्टोबरमासस्य त्रिंशे दिनाङ्के रानुल्फः अपुलियानृपतिः रिञ्ञानोयुद्धे रोजरद्वितीयं शिशिलीयराजानं पराजयत्। (On October 30, 1137, Ranulf, lord of Apulia, defeated Roger II, king of Sicily, at the Battle of Rignano.)
- रोजरस्य सेना भग्ना अभवत्; राजा स्वयं बन्धनात् कथञ्चन मुक्तः, तस्य शिबिरं च लुण्ठितम्। (Roger's army was broken; the king himself barely escaped capture, and his camp was plundered.)
- अनेन विजयेन रानुल्फस्य अपुलियादुकपदवी दृढा अभवत्, परन्तु द्विवर्षानन्तरं तस्य मृत्युः संवृत्ता। (By this victory Ranulf's position as Duke of Apulia was secured, but his death came two years later.)

## Simple Sanskrit notes for learners — Theme: Warfare and Armies (युद्धम्)

- युद्धम् (yuddham) — battle, war; literally "a fighting." [battle / 战斗]
- सेना (senā) — army; literally "that which bears weapons." [army / 军队]
- विजयः (vijayaḥ) — victory, triumph; literally "complete conquest." [victory / 胜利]

## English

- On October 30, 1137, Ranulf of Apulia defeated King Roger II of Sicily at the Battle of Rignano, one of the sharpest Norman defeats in southern Italy.
- Roger II's army broke and fled; the king himself barely escaped capture while his camp was plundered by the victors.
- The victory secured Ranulf's position as Duke of Apulia, but he died only two years later, and Roger eventually absorbed Apulia into his kingdom.

## Chinese

- 公元1137年10月30日，阿普利亚的拉努尔夫在里尼亚诺战役中击败西西里国王罗杰二世，这是诺曼人在南意大利遭遇的最惨重失败之一。
- 罗杰二世的军队溃败而逃，国王本人险些被俘，营地遭胜利者洗劫。
- 这场胜利巩固了拉努尔夫的阿普利亚公爵地位，但他两年后去世，罗杰二世最终将阿普利亚并入自己的王国。
