## Simple Sanskrit notes for learners — Theme: Conspiracy and Treason

- षड्यन्त्रम् (ṣaḍyantram) — conspiracy, secret plot; literal sense: that which is devised or contrived (in secret). [conspiracy / 阴谋]
- राजद्रोहः (rājadrohaḥ) — treason against the king; literal sense: hostility or injury toward the ruler. [treason / 叛国]
- विस्फोटः (visphoṭaḥ) — explosion, blast; literal sense: a bursting forth. [explosion / 爆炸]

## English

- On November 5, 1605, Guy Fawkes was arrested in the cellars of the Houses of Parliament, where he had planted gunpowder intended to blow up the building and kill King James I of England.
- The Gunpowder Plot exposed the deep religious fractures of post-Reformation England: a small band of Catholic conspirators hoped that decapitating the Protestant state in one blast would restore their faith to power.
- The lesson is that states survive conspiracy through vigilance and intelligence — and that failed treason can echo for centuries, as Britain's annual Bonfire Night still shows.

## Chinese

- 公元1605年11月5日,盖伊·福克斯在英国议会大厦地下室被捕,他在那里埋下了火药,企图炸毁大厦并杀死英格兰国王詹姆斯一世。
- 火药阴谋暴露了宗教改革后英格兰深刻的宗教裂痕:一小群天主教密谋者希望用一次爆炸斩首新教国家,从而恢复天主教的统治地位。
- 教训是:国家依靠警惕与情报工作才能在阴谋中幸存,而失败的叛国行为也会在记忆中回响数百年——英国一年一度的篝火之夜至今仍在证明这一点。
