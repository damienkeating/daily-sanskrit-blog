## Sanskrit news

- ११७९ क्रिस्ताब्दे नवेम्बरमासस्य प्रथमे दिनाङ्के रेम्सनगरे फिलिप्पद्वितीयः फ्रान्सदेशस्य राजा अभिषिक्तः। (On November 1, 1179, at Reims, Philip II was crowned King of France.)
- तदा सः चतुर्दशवर्षीयः आसीत्; पितरि लुडोविकसप्तमे जीवति एव अभिषेकः अभवत्, पश्चात् सः "अगस्ट" इति प्रसिद्धः। (He was fourteen then; the coronation happened while his father Louis VII was still alive, and later he became famous as "Augustus.")
- तस्य शासने फ्रान्सदेशः बलवान् अभवत्, राजशक्तिः वर्धिता, च बौविन्युद्धे महान् विजयः प्राप्तः। (During his reign France grew powerful, royal authority expanded, and a great victory was won at the Battle of Bouvines.)

## Simple Sanskrit notes for learners — Theme: Coronation and Kingship (राज्याभिषेकः)

- राज्याभिषेकः (rājyābhiṣekaḥ) — royal coronation; literally "the anointing/sprinkling into kingship." [coronation / 加冕]
- मुकुटः (mukuṭaḥ) — crown, diadem; literally "a crest or head ornament." [crown / 王冠]
- सिंहासनम् (siṃhāsanam) — throne; literally "lion seat." [throne / 王座]

## English

- On November 1, 1179, Philip II was crowned King of France at Reims Cathedral, an early coronation arranged while his ailing father Louis VII was still alive.
- Crowned at only fourteen, Philip — later honored as "Augustus" — spent his 43-year reign breaking the power of rival feudal lords, above all the Angevin kings of England.
- His triumph at Bouvines in 1214 and the vast expansion of royal lands made France the leading power of medieval Europe, showing how a boy crowned young could grow into a ruler of iron will.

## Chinese

- 公元1179年11月1日，腓力二世在兰斯大教堂加冕为法国国王，当时其父路易七世仍在世，故这是一次提前举行的加冕。
- 加冕时年仅十四岁的腓力——后来被尊称为"奥古斯都"——在长达43年的统治中不断削弱封建诸侯，尤其是英格兰金雀花王朝的势力。
- 1214年布汶战役的胜利和王室领地的大幅扩张，使法国成为中世纪欧洲的强权，展现了一位少年加冕的国王如何成长为铁腕君主。
