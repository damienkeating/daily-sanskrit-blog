## Sanskrit news
- २९ सेप्टेम्बरे ८५५ तमे वर्षे प्रजानां विद्रोहेण तृतीयः बेनेडिक्टस् पोप् पुनः पादपीठे स्थापितः।
- इटलीराजः द्वितीयः लोदोविकस् तं विहाय स्वप्रियं पुरोहितं स्थापयितुं यतमानो जनाक्रोशात् विफलः अभवत्।
- इदं दर्शयति यत् प्रजाशक्तिः नृपतिच्छायामपि धर्मपीठं रक्षितुं शक्नोति।

## Simple Sanskrit notes for learners — Theme: People & Power
- प्रजा (prajā) — the people, subjects; literally "those born forth (under a ruler)". [people / 民众]
- विद्रोहः (vidrohaḥ) — uprising, rebellion; literally "the act of rising against". [uprising / 起义]
- पुनर्स्थापना (punarsthāpanā) — restoration, reinstating; literally "the act of setting up again". [restoration / 复位]

## English
- On September 29, 855, Pope Benedict III was restored to the papacy by a popular uprising in Rome.
- King Louis II of Italy had tried to replace him with his own favourite candidate, but the Roman people rejected the imposed rival and forced the legitimate pope's return.
- The episode shows that medieval legitimacy rested not only on royal favour but also on the consent of the people, whose collective action could overturn a king's design.

## Chinese
- 855年9月29日，教皇本笃三世在罗马民众起义的支持下重登教皇之位。
- 意大利国王路易二世曾试图以亲信候选人取代他，但罗马民众拒绝了强加的对手，迫使合法教皇回归。
- 此事表明，中世纪的合法性不仅依赖王权的支持，也取决于民众的认同——民众的集体行动足以推翻君主的图谋。
