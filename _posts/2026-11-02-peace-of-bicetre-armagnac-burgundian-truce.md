## Simple Sanskrit notes for learners — Theme: Peace and Truce

- सन्धिः (sandhiḥ) — treaty, truce; literal sense: a joining together of two sides. [treaty / 条约]
- शान्तिः (śāntiḥ) — peace; literal sense: the calming of strife and noise. [peace / 和平]
- मैत्री (maitrī) — friendship, amity; literal sense: the bond between allies. [friendship / 友谊]

## English

- On November 2, 1410, the Peace of Bicêtre suspended hostilities in the Armagnac–Burgundian Civil War that had torn France apart.
- The truce exposed how deeply France's royal factions had divided the kingdom while England threatened from outside during the Hundred Years' War.
- The lesson is that negotiated pauses, even temporary ones, can save lives and open space for lasting settlements.
