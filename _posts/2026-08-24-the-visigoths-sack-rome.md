**Daily Sanskrit learning - history's today: 24/08/2026**

**Today's theme:** Invasion and Conquest / 入侵与征服

### Sanskrit news
- संवत्सरे ४१० क्रि.श. विसिगोथानां राजा अलारिकः रोमनगरं लुण्ठितवान्।
- अनेन आक्रमणेन रोमीयसाम्राज्यस्य दुर्बलता प्रकटिता अभवत्।
- अतः बलवद्रक्षणं सर्वदा आवश्यकम् इति पाठः।

### Simple Sanskrit notes for learners — Theme: Invasion and Conquest
- **लुण्ठनम्** (luṇṭhanam) — Plundering, sacking; literal sense: looting. [plunder / 掠夺]
- **आक्रमणम्** (ākramaṇam) — Attack, invasion; literal sense: stepping upon. [attack / 入侵]
- **साम्राज्यम्** (sāmrājyam) — Empire, imperial rule; literal sense: universal sovereignty. [empire / 帝国]

### English
- In 410 AD, the Visigoths under King Alaric I began to sack Rome, marking a pivotal moment in the decline of the Western Roman Empire.
- This invasion exposed the vulnerability of a once-invincible empire that had relied too heavily on external mercenaries.
- The lesson remains that even the mightiest civilizations require robust internal defense and unity.

### Chinese
- 公元410年，西哥特人在国王阿拉里克一世的领导下开始洗劫罗马，这是西罗马帝国衰落的关键时刻。
- 这次入侵暴露了一个曾经不可一世的帝国的脆弱性，该帝国过于依赖外部雇佣兵。
- 教训是，即使是最强大的文明也需要强大的内部防御和团结。
