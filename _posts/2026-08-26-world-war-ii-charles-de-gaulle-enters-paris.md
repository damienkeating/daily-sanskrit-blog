## Sanskrit news
- २६ अगस्ते १९४४ तमे वर्षे चार्ल्स् डि गॉल् पेरिसं प्रविष्टवान्।
- द्वितीये विश्वयुद्धे नाझिसेनाया मुक्तिः अभवत्।
- एषः विजयस्य महान् दिवसः आसीत्।

## Simple Sanskrit notes for learners — Theme: Liberation & Victory
- मुक्तिः (muktiḥ) — liberation; literally "release or freedom". [liberation / 解放]
- विजयः (vijayaḥ) — victory; literally "conquest or triumph". [victory / 胜利]
- युद्धम् (yuddham) — war; literally "battle or conflict". [war / 战争]

## English
- On August 26, 1944, during World War II, General Charles de Gaulle entered Paris following its liberation from Nazi occupation.
- This historic moment symbolized the restoration of French sovereignty and the turning tide against Axis powers in Europe.
- The liberation of Paris remains a powerful reminder of resilience, leadership, and the enduring struggle for freedom.
