## Sanskrit news
- २७ सेप्टेम्बरे १९१६ तमे वर्षे इयासु पञ्चमः इथियोपियायाः राज्यात् भ्रष्टः घोषितः।
- प्रासादान्तर्गते राजनीतिके परिवर्तने तस्य काकी जेव्दितु नूतना शासिका अभवत्।
- राज्यपरिवर्तनं सदा जनानां जीवनं बध्नाति।

## Simple Sanskrit notes for learners — Theme: Power & Succession
- राज्यम् (rājyam) — kingdom, realm; literally "that which is ruled". [kingdom / 王国]
- राज्ञी (rājñī) — queen, female ruler; literally "she who reigns". [queen / 女王]
- अधिकारः (adhikāraḥ) — authority, power; literally "rightful claim or command". [power / 权力]

## English
- On September 27, 1916, Iyasu V was proclaimed deposed as ruler of Ethiopia in a palace coup in favor of his aunt Zewditu.
- The coup exposed deep tensions between the Ethiopian nobility, the church, and the young heir, whose wartime leanings had alarmed the establishment.
- Zewditu's accession showed that political legitimacy rests on the support of powerful institutions, not on birthright alone.

## Chinese

- 这场政变暴露了贵族、教会与这位年轻继承人之间的深刻矛盾——他在战时的立场令统治集团深感不安。
- 佐迪图的登基表明，政治合法性不仅取决于血统，更取决于强大机构的支持。
