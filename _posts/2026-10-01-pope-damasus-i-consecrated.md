Daily Sanskrit learning - history's today: 01/10/2026
Today's theme: Sacred Authority / 神圣权威

## Sanskrit news
- संवत्सरे ३६६ तमे दमाससः प्रथमः रोमन-धर्माचार्यः अभिषिक्तः अभवत्। (saṁvatsare 366 tame damāsasaḥ prathamaḥ romana-dharmācāryaḥ abhiṣiktaḥ abhavat.)
- तस्य अभिषेके पुरोहिताः धर्मस्य शुद्धतां नीतिं च समीक्षितवन्तः। (tasya abhiṣeke purohitāḥ dharmasya śuddhatāṁ nītiṁ ca samīkṣitavantaḥ.)
- एषः कालः दर्शयति यत् धार्मिकः आदेशः जनानां विश्वासं नेतृत्वं च गठयति। (eṣaḥ kālaḥ darśayati yat dhārmikaḥ ādeśaḥ janānāṁ viśvāsaṁ netṛtvaṁ ca gaṭhayati.)

## Simple Sanskrit notes for learners — Theme: Sacred Authority
- पुरोहितः (purohitaḥ) — priest, one placed in front of the sacred; literal sense "he who is set before". [Priest/祭司]
- धर्मः (dharmaḥ) — sacred duty, divine law; literal sense "that which upholds". [Law/法则]
- आशीर्वादः (āśīrvādaḥ) — blessing, consecration through prayer; literal sense "speech invoking grace". [Blessing/祝福]

## English
- In 366 AD, Damasus I was consecrated as Bishop of Rome, becoming one of the most influential early popes.
- His election exposed deep divisions within the Church, with rival claimants and street violence revealing how fragile ecclesiastical authority still was.
- His papacy reminds us that institutional legitimacy must be earned through unity, doctrine, and service rather than force.

## Chinese
- 公元366年，达马苏斯一世被祝圣为罗马主教，成为早期最具影响力的教皇之一。
- 他的当选暴露了教会内部的深刻分裂，敌对候选人之间的冲突乃至街头暴力显示出教会权威仍然脆弱。
- 他的教皇任期提醒我们，机构的合法性必须依靠团结、教义和服务来赢得，而非武力。
