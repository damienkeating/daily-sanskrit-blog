- **धर्मः** (dharmaḥ) — religion, duty, law; that which upholds. religion / 宗教
- **न्यायः** (nyāyaḥ) — justice, logic; method of reasoning. justice / 正义
- **दण्डः** (daṇḍaḥ) — punishment, penalty; rod, stick. punishment / 惩罚

## English

- In 1559, Spanish archbishop Bartolomé Carranza was arrested for heresy by the Spanish Inquisition.
- His arrest demonstrated the immense power of religious authorities in 16th-century Spain.
- The case reminds us that conflicts between religious doctrine and state authority often produce grave consequences.
