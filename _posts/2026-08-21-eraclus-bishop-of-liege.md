## Sanskrit news

- ९५९ तमे संवत्सरे एराक्लुस् लीज्-नगरस्य पञ्चविंशतितमः पुरोहितः अभवत्।
- एषा नियुक्तिः मध्ययुगीययुरोपे धार्मिकनेतृत्वस्य महत्त्वं सूचयति।
- तेन कार्येण तत् क्षेत्रं धार्मिकसांस्कृतिकविकासेन समृद्धम् अभवत्।

## Simple Sanskrit notes for learners — Theme: Religious Leadership

- धर्मः (dharmaḥ) — religion, sacred duty; that which upholds or supports. [religion / 宗教]
- पुरोहितः (purohitaḥ) — priest, prelate; one placed in front (of sacred rites). [priest / 祭司]
- नियुक्तिः (niyuktiḥ) — appointment, installation; being attached to duty. [appointment / 任命]

## English

- In 959 AD, Eraclus became the 25th bishop of Liège, a prominent religious center in medieval Europe.
- This appointment underscored the profound influence of ecclesiastical leadership on community and governance.
- His tenure enriched the region with spiritual guidance and cultural development for generations.

## Chinese

- 公元959年，埃拉克鲁斯成为列日第25任主教，列日是中世纪欧洲重要的宗教中心。
- 这一任命突显了教会领袖对社区与治理的深远影响。
- 他的任期为该地区带来了精神指引和文化发展，惠及后世。
