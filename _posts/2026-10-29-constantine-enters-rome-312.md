## Sanskrit news

- ३१२ क्रिस्ताब्दे कुश्तन्तीनसम्राट् मिल्वियसेतोः युद्धे विजयं प्राप्य रोमनगरं प्रविष्टवान्। (In 312 CE, Emperor Constantine, having won victory at the Battle of the Milvian Bridge, entered the city of Rome.)
- जनाः महता हर्षेण तं सम्मानयामासुः; मक्सेन्तियोः शवः टिबेरिनद्याः उद्धृतः शिरश्छेदेन दण्डितश्च। (The people honored him with great jubilation; Maxentius's body was pulled from the Tiber and punished by beheading.)
- अयं विजयः रोमसाम्राज्ये ईसायीधर्मस्य पथं प्रशस्तवान्। (This victory paved the way for Christianity in the Roman Empire.)

## Simple Sanskrit notes for learners — Theme: Victory and Triumph (विजयः)

- विजयः (vijayaḥ) — victory, triumph; literally "complete conquest." [victory / 胜利]
- प्रवेशः (praveśaḥ) — entry, entrance; literally "a going into." [entry / 进入]
- हर्षः (harṣaḥ) — joy, jubilation; literally "bristling with delight." [jubilation / 欢庆]

## English

- On October 29, 312, Constantine the Great entered Rome after defeating his rival Maxentius at the Battle of the Milvian Bridge, staging a grand adventus and being met with popular jubilation.
- The victory ended a civil war and left Constantine master of the Western Roman Empire, while Maxentius's body was fished out of the Tiber and beheaded.
- The triumph set the stage for the Edict of Milan in 313 and the eventual Christianization of the Roman Empire.

## Chinese

- 公元312年10月29日，君士坦丁大帝在米尔维安桥战役击败马克森提乌斯后进入罗马，举行了盛大的入城仪式，民众欢欣鼓舞。
- 这场胜利结束了内战，使君士坦丁成为西罗马帝国的主宰；马克森提乌斯的尸体从台伯河中被打捞上来并被斩首。
- 这一胜利为313年的《米兰敕令》以及罗马帝国最终的基督教化铺平了道路。
