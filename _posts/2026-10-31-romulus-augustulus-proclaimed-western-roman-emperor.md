## Sanskrit news

- ४७५ क्रिस्ताब्दे अक्टोबरमासस्य एकत्रिंशे दिनाङ्के रोमुलस् अगस्टुलस् पश्चिमरोमसाम्राज्यस्य सम्राट् उद्घोषितः अभवत्। (On October 31, 475, Romulus Augustulus was proclaimed emperor of the Western Roman Empire.)
- तस्य पिता ओरेस्टस् सेनापतिः आसीत्; पूर्वसम्राट् जुलियस् नेपोस् पलायितः, बालराजा केवलं नाममात्रस्य शासकः। (His father Orestes was the general; the former emperor Julius Nepos fled, and the boy-king was ruler in name only.)
- एकादशमासानन्तरं जर्मननायकः ओदोवकरः तं निर्वासयत् — पश्चिमरोमसाम्राज्यस्य अन्तिमः पतनम् आसीत्। (Eleven months later the Germanic leader Odoacer deposed him — this was the fall of the Western Roman Empire.)

## Simple Sanskrit notes for learners — Theme: Empire and Kingship (साम्राज्यम्)

- राजा (rājā) — king; literally "one who shines or rules." [king / 国王]
- साम्राज्यम् (sāmrājyam) — empire; literally "the dominion of a sovereign." [empire / 帝国]
- पतनम् (patanam) — fall, collapse; literally "a falling down." [fall / 衰落]

## English

- On October 31, 475, Romulus Augustulus was proclaimed emperor of the Western Roman Empire by his father, the general Orestes, after the previous emperor Julius Nepos was driven into exile.
- The young ruler — his nickname "Augustulus" mockingly meant "little Augustus" — held power in name only, while real authority lay with his father's army.
- Barely eleven months later the Germanic chieftain Odoacer deposed him, an act conventionally marking the end of the Western Roman Empire and the start of the Middle Ages in Europe.

## Chinese

- 公元475年10月31日，罗慕路斯·奥古斯都路斯被其父、将军奥瑞斯特斯拥立为西罗马皇帝，前任皇帝朱利乌斯·尼波斯被迫流亡。
- 这位年幼的统治者——绰号"奥古斯都路斯"意为"小奥古斯都"——只是名义上的皇帝，实权掌握在父亲的军队手中。
- 仅仅十一个月后，日耳曼首领奥多亚克废黜了他，这一事件通常被视为西罗马帝国灭亡和欧洲中世纪的开端。
