## Simple Sanskrit notes for learners — Theme: Fraternal Conflict

- भ्राता (bhrātā) — brother; literal sense: one who supports or upholds the family line. [brother / 兄弟]
- आक्रमणम् (ākramaṇam) — attack, assault; literal sense: a stepping upon or marching over another's ground. [attack / 进攻]
- पराजयः (parājayaḥ) — defeat; literal sense: being thoroughly conquered away. [defeat / 失败]

## English

- On November 3, 1090, the Rouen Riot unfolded: English king William Rufus's attempt to take possession of Rouen, the capital city of his brother Robert, Duke of Normandy, failed.
- The rising in the city and the collapsed plot exposed the bitter dynastic rivalry among the sons of William the Conqueror over England and Normandy.
- The lesson is that seizing power through kin-sabotage often backfires, deepening family enmity instead of securing rule.

- 这场未遂的政变暴露了征服者威廉诸子之间围绕英格兰与诺曼底统治权的激烈王朝之争。
